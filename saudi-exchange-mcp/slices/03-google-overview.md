## Goal
Given a pilot company's Arabic/English name and/or ticker, resolve the **existing** shared company identity from slice 01 and, through a documented, revision-pinned reuse of [woodstock-tokyo/google-finance-mcp](https://github.com/woodstock-tokyo/google-finance-mcp), return a verified Google Finance **overview** (quote plus associated statistics), **profile/about**, and **company news** for that same company. Attach verified Google quote identifiers to the existing identity records (Saudi Aramco `2222`, Maaden `1211`); do not create a second company identity. Hide changing Google dataset keys (`ds:N`, live RPC hashes) behind stable capabilities. Keep Google responses transient. This is the Google research branch at library/CLI level; earnings and financial-statement tables remain slice 04; MCP host/client install remains slice 05.
